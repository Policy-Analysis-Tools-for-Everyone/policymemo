## 18 November 2026 · 0.2.1

- New: say "report this session" at any point and policymemo.ai drafts a session report from the conversation, goes through it with you to take out anything sensitive, and gives you a link to send it, either as a public GitHub issue or as a private email. Nothing is sent until you press Submit or Send.

## 5 October 2026 · 0.2.0

- New: policymemo.ai for ChatGPT, as a plugin holding 10 skills.
- The install page now covers Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini, in that order.

## 4 October 2026 · 0.2.0

- The private beta opens. This is the version beta testers install.
- The session report asks which version, which AI tool, and whether it was your first time, so reports can be matched to what you ran.

## 4 October 2026 · 0.1.2

- New: policymemo.ai for Microsoft 365 Copilot, as 8 custom skills.
- New: policymemo.ai for Google Gemini, as 10 standalone skills. This replaces the earlier Gem.
- New: one install page for Claude, Copilot and Gemini.
- The skills now follow their own writing rules when they draft documents.
- Caveats and contested framings are carried into finished documents, not dropped at the drafting stage.
- Revised the house rules and the problem skill, using evidence from the alpha.
